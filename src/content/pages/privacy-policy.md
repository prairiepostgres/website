We take your privacy and our responsibility toward it seriously.

When you visit our website or the websites of events we run, we automatically log your IP address.

When you complete a form or survey, or apply to become a member of Prairie Postgres, we will retain the information you submit to us. This information may include your name, email address, and state of residence.

We collect this information to develop our programming, improve our website and communications, and to contact people for technical and support purposes. We will not share your contact information with any other organizations unless required to by law.
